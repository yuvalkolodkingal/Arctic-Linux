# Arctic Linux showcase

## Style prompt
Use Arctic Linux's existing brand book in ../design/brand-book.md. A calm, keyboard-first desktop inspired by the arctic fox. Show the real repository screens at a generous size, with plain sentence-case chapter titles and useful shortcuts. The video begins in Polar night and visits Winter during the appearance chapter. Use restrained fades and short slides, soft corners, generous space, and warm, clear narration.

## Colors
- Polar night ground: #12171e; surface: #1a212a; raised surface: #232b36.
- Polar night primary text: #e9eef3; secondary text: #aeb9c5; hairline: #2f3945.
- Winter ground: #eef2f5; surface: #fbfcfd; primary text: #151a21; secondary text: #4a5663.
- Amber #f6bd55 (Polar night) / #efa637 (Winter), exclusively for active progress, focus, selected state, and the fox's eyes.
- Warm neutral: #c9bcad on #2f2924.

## Typography
Figtree 400, 500, 600, 700 for titles and body. JetBrains Mono 400 for shortcuts and technical labels. Embed the repository's OFL font files locally. Video headings use 64–88px; supporting text uses 26–32px; captions use 28px.

## Motion
Crossfade scenes over 0.6 seconds. Every scene's title, description, shortcut, and screen have distinct entrances using opacity and slides of no more than 8px. No bounce, parallax, or camera shake. Screens remain stable for reading. Final scene may fade to the background.

## What not to do
- No invented OS screens, simulated live application activity, or fabricated installation success.
- No amber headings, decoration, large backgrounds, or white labels on amber.
- No gradients on cards or controls; aurora imagery comes from the actual OS wallpapers.
- No flashy transitions, jargon in the viewer-facing copy, or excessive small text.
- Do not describe sample installer data or test fixtures as a newly performed installation.
